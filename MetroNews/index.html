NORTH SHORE

North Shore Emergency Management Unveils Updated Earthquake Preparedness Plan

Staff Reporter — Feb 26, 2026

The North Shore Emergency Management Office released its 2026 Community Resilience Plan this week, outlining expanded cache locations, a new neighbourhood alert network, and updated evacuation routes for the Sea-to-Sky corridor. Residents are encouraged to register their households in the voluntary registry before April 1.

Officials cited the 2024 seismic study of the Burrard Inlet fault zone as a key motivator for the upgraded timeline. Community information sessions will be held in March at North Vancouver Rec Centre and West Van Community Centre.

HOUSING

Affordable Housing Units Approved for Lower Lonsdale Redevelopment

Staff Reporter — Feb 25, 2026

North Vancouver City Council gave third reading to a rezoning application that will see 340 new units built along the Lonsdale corridor, with 20% designated below-market rental. The project is expected to break ground in Q3 2026. Critics have raised concerns about shadow impacts on adjacent properties while housing advocates celebrated the approval.

ENVIRONMENT

Lynn Creek Restoration Project Enters Final Phase

Staff Reporter — Feb 24, 2026

The District of North Vancouver confirmed that the Lynn Creek restoration project — a decade-long initiative to restore riparian habitat and reduce flood risk — has entered its final planting phase. Over 12,000 native plants will be installed along the lower creek before summer. The project has already seen the return of coho salmon to previously blocked spawning areas.

MOUNTAIN SPORTS

Grouse Mountain Reports Record Snowpack for Late February

Feb 25, 2026

Grouse Mountain's snowpack is sitting at 210cm at the summit — well above the 30-year average of 165cm for late February. The mountain has extended its seasonal pass sales and is projecting operations through at least mid-April. North Shore skiers are being reminded to carry avalanche gear on backcountry routes.
