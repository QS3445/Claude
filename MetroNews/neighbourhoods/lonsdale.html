City of North Vancouver

Lonsdale &
Lower Lonsdale

The urban heart of the North Shore. From the SeaBus terminal at Lonsdale Quay to the boutique shops of Central Lonsdale, this dense, walkable corridor is North Van's fastest-growing neighbourhood — blending waterfront living with a thriving arts, food, and transit scene.
