District of North Vancouver

Deep Cove

Nestled at the end of Indian Arm, Deep Cove is one of the North Shore's most beloved villages — famed for its kayaking, hiking trails, the iconic Honey Doughnuts queue, and an unhurried pace of life surrounded by towering Douglas firs and the still waters of the fjord. A destination for outdoor adventurers and a sanctuary for residents who chose it deliberately.

Local News

PLANNING

District Receives 600+ Responses to Deep Cove Community Plan Survey

Feb 22, 2026

The District of North Vancouver's public engagement for the Deep Cove Community Plan Update attracted over 600 responses in its opening two weeks, well above the typical 150–200 for comparable surveys. Top themes: parking management in summer, preservation of village-scale architecture, and improved pedestrian connections to Cates Park and Indian Arm Provincial Park.

A second round of engagement — focused on specific policy options — is scheduled for April. Residents can participate at dnv.org or at the Community Centre in March.

OUTDOOR RECREATION

Mount Seymour Ski Area Extends Season to Mid-April on Record Snowpack

Feb 25, 2026

Mount Seymour Resort confirmed it will operate through at least April 19, 2026, citing a 210cm summit snowpack — the highest for late February in 18 years. The decision is welcomed by Deep Cove residents and businesses who benefit from increased winter visitor traffic. The resort is also piloting a new car-free shuttle from Phibbs Exchange.

ENVIRONMENT

Coho Salmon Return to Rilling Creek for First Time in Eight Years

Feb 18, 2026

Volunteers from the Deep Cove Watershed Society confirmed coho salmon were observed spawning in Rilling Creek this winter for the first time since 2018, following a multi-year habitat restoration project. The return is attributed to sediment removal, log-jam placement, and a ban on off-trail vehicles that had damaged the streambed.
