District of West Vancouver

Dundarave &
Ambleside

West Vancouver's beachfront villages — Dundarave and Ambleside sit side by side along Marine Drive, offering sandy beaches, a public pier, independent boutiques, and relaxed waterfront dining. Beloved by walkers, cyclists, and families, the area retains a small-town village feel even as it sits at the crossroads of significant redevelopment pressure.
