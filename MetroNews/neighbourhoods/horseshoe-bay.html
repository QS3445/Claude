District of West Vancouver

Horseshoe Bay

Where the city meets the sea — and the ferry. Horseshoe Bay is simultaneously a tight-knit village of 2,500 residents and British Columbia's busiest coastal ferry terminal, welcoming travellers bound for Nanaimo, Bowen Island, and the Sunshine Coast. Fish and chips on the dock, sea lions on the rocks, and the daily drama of boats arriving and departing.
