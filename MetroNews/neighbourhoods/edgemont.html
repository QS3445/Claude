District of North Vancouver

Edgemont Village

Perched at the foot of Grouse Mountain, Edgemont is one of North Vancouver's most coveted family neighbourhoods. Its compact village core on Edgemont Boulevard is lined with independent bookshops, bakeries, and restaurants, while the surrounding streets offer quiet residential living with quick trail access to the North Shore mountains.

Local News

COMMUNITY

Edgemont BIA Completes $400K Streetscape Renewal on Edgemont Boulevard

Feb 20, 2026

The Edgemont Village Business Improvement Association has wrapped up a $400,000 streetscape renewal project on Edgemont Boulevard, installing new heritage-style lamp posts, stone-inlaid planters, additional benches, and widened sidewalk patio space. The project was co-funded by the District of North Vancouver and Village merchants.

Village businesses report a 12% increase in foot traffic since temporary construction hoarding came down in January. A ribbon-cutting ceremony is planned for the March long weekend.

EDUCATION

Windsor Secondary Launches AP Data Science and Computer Science Programs

Feb 21, 2026

Windsor Secondary School has been approved by SD44 to introduce Advanced Placement Data Science and Computer Science courses for the 2026–27 school year, the first public school in North Vancouver to offer both. The programs are expected to attract enrolment from across the district and will be supported by a new computer lab funded through a partnership with Capilano University.

New Inclusive Playground Approved for Rabbits Den Park

Feb 16, 2026

District of North Vancouver Council approved a $280,000 inclusive playground upgrade at Rabbits Den Park on Edgemont Boulevard. The new equipment — designed for children of all abilities including those using mobility devices — will replace aging structures installed in 2001. Construction begins April 15 with completion by Canada Day.
